Behind the numbers

Why I built Theater Spotlight

I came to Broadway for the stories. My curiosity kept going after the curtain fell.

Musicals have always been a source of energy and inspiration for me. As a data scientist, I began wondering what was happening beyond the stage: how ticket prices and weekly grosses move, how full different theatres become, and what recognition at the Tony Awards can—and cannot—tell us about a show's momentum.

Theater Spotlight turns that curiosity into a transparent data project. The dashboards describe the market, the Tony study examines stronger comparisons and uncertainty, and the modeling work asks what can be forecast from the signals we already have. That includes a closing-risk tracker that estimates the probability a production closes within eight weeks. The goal is not to reduce a musical to a number, but to use data to understand the business that keeps the stories on stage.
